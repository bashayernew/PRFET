### English
```
PRFET is an 18+ social app that brings everything you need to connect into one place.

Discover people around you, make new friends, and share your moments — with privacy controls that keep you in charge.

Features:
• Profile & feed: post photos, videos and stories, and follow the people you care about.
• Chat & calls: private messages, voice notes, and 1:1 voice and video calls.
• Live audio rooms: join live discussion rooms or host your own.
• Nearby discovery: find people and businesses near you (via location and Bluetooth), with your consent.
• AI assistant: chat with a smart assistant, generate images and videos, and get content ideas.
• Jobs & ads: post a job or promote your business to thousands of users.
• Private vault: keep your private media safe.
• Privacy controls: close your inbox, allow only who you choose, and block or report any user.

Subscriptions:
• Golden and VIP: higher AI limits, precise location sharing, and extra perks.
• Add-on packs for more images, videos and storage.

PRFET is for adults only (18+). We respect your privacy and never sell your data.
Privacy policy: https://prfet.com/privacy
```
